⚡ PixelSqueeze — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

PixelSqueeze ("we", "our", or "the app") is a high-performance, offline image compression, resizing, and format conversion application developed by Gunaseelan N (ANGS). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your photos and data.

🔒 100% On-Device & Offline Privacy: PixelSqueeze processes all photos, images, and documents locally on your device hardware. Your images are NEVER uploaded, stored, or transferred to any cloud servers or third parties.

1. Information We Access and Process Locally

PixelSqueeze accesses only the device features required to perform image compression and editing:

  • Photo Library & Selected Images: When you pick an image to compress, resize, or convert, PixelSqueeze reads the file temporarily into memory to process it. Once saved or discarded, the working temporary cache is automatically cleaned.
  • Camera (Optional): If you choose to capture a new photo directly within the app for scanning or compression.
  • App Preferences: Your selected preferences (theme, language, default compression quality, history) are saved locally on your device using encrypted local storage.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or store your photos, documents, or personal files on external servers.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.
  • We do not track your browsing activity or sell personal data to third parties.

3. Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

PixelSqueeze is a free utility application supported by advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve advertisements and prevent ad fraud, Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / IDFA)
  • App performance, diagnostics, and crash logs
  • IP address (used by Google to infer general geographic region for ad delivery)

Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

4. App Permissions Explained

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Enables you to select images from your gallery for compression.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Enables saving compressed, resized images and PDF files directly to your device storage (Android 10 and below).
  • CAMERA (Optional): Allows taking photos directly inside the app.

5. Children's Privacy

PixelSqueeze is intended for general audiences (aged 13 and above) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
